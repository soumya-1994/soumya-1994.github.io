About

Dr. Soumya Ranjan Mohanty is a Project Research Scientist-I on an ICMR Extramural Grant project in Varanasi and a doctoral researcher in the Department of Zoology, Institute of Science, Banaras Hindu University. He has more than six years of research experience in the biological sciences, which began as an ICMR Junior and Senior Research Fellow in 2020. His work looks at circadian desynchronization and its effects on pregnancy, metabolism and reproductive health, mainly in the golden (Syrian) hamster. He has also contributed to studies on melatonin, nanoparticle-based anti-inflammatory therapy and anticancer metal complexes.

Since 2022 he has taught and supervised six M.Sc. Zoology dissertation students at BHU, guiding each through a six-month research project.

Research

Current Project · ICMR Extramural Grant

ICMR Extramural Research Project

Studying how circadian desynchronization affects metabolic and reproductive health during pregnancy, using hamster models. Recent output from this work: a first-author article in Physiology & Behavior (Elsevier, 2026) showing that circadian desynchronization induces diabetes-like conditions in gestational female Syrian hamsters.

Funded by: Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) PI: Dr. Sanjeev Kumar Yadav, Dept. of Zoology, BHU July 2024 – Present

Research Interests

Chronobiology

Effects of circadian disruption and long-term light exposure on physiology, including Akt/FoxO1 signalling.

Reproductive Physiology

Pregnancy outcomes, testicular inflammation and fertility in hamster models.

Metabolic Disorders

Gestational diabetes-like conditions arising from circadian desynchronization.

Melatonin & Therapeutics

Melatonin as an oncostatic agent, plant-derived nanoparticles and anticancer metal complexes.

Toxicology

Toxicity studies in rodent models, recognised with a Young Scientist Award (2024).
